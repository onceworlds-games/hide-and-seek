# Hide and Seek

Hide in the house before the seeker opens their eyes. Hide inside wardrobes, under beds and in boxes, or keep running out of
the flashlight's beam. Found players join the seekers. Survive the clock to win.

Play it at https://onceworlds.com/play/hide-and-seek

Move with WASD or the arrow keys (or the on-screen stick), Space is Hide or Search. Seekers only see what their flashlight
lights. 2 to 10 players, bots fill the empty seats. A plain-JavaScript game (Canvas 2D, no build); `npm test` runs the rules,
vision, collision and bot tests. MIT licensed.
